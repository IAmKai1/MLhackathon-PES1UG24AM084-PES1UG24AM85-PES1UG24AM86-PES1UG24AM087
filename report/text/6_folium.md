All maps are generated by pathsnap/maps.py from the pipeline's actual inputs and outputs. Each Folium map has four layers with distinct colours: raw GPS observations as blue points, the road network near the trajectory as thin grey lines, the reference route as a thick, semi-transparent green line, and the predicted route as an orange line. A legend in the lower left names each layer and shows the trip's accuracy; a layer control in the upper right switches layers on and off. The view automatically fits the trajectory's extent.

The maps were essential during development. The EDA map of training trip 54 is what revealed that its reference route stops matching the GPS after the first part of the drive, which led to the trusted-label rule. On student-testing trip 77 the predicted route overlays the reference route almost everywhere, and the remaining differences sit at a few junctions and short side streets, matching the error analysis in Section 5.

Interactive maps for every student-testing trip are in results/maps/, for every evaluator trip in results/evaluator/, and for selected EDA trips in results/eda/. They can be opened in any browser.
